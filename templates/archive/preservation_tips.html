Preservation Tips

How to protect, digitize, and archive furry convention conbooks and materials for future generations.

Why Save Conbooks?

  • Conbooks are the black box of our community—primary sources for future historians.
  • They record who we were, what we built, and what mattered to us.
  • Most are rare, fragile, and easily lost to time or neglect.
  • The majority of leftover copies are usually thrown out instead of given away, making surviving originals even rarer.

Every copy matters. Every year matters. Every page is history.

How to Preserve

  • Store in a cool, dry place: Avoid attics, basements, and direct sunlight.
  • Keep flat and supported: Store books upright or flat, not slanted or bent.
  • Handle with clean hands: Oils and dirt can damage pages and covers.
  • Watch for rust: Staples can rust and stain paper—consider archival sleeves or polyester strips.
  • Digitize when possible: Scan or photograph rare or damaged books to preserve their content.

Donating & Sharing

  • Have a rare or old conbook? Consider donating or lending it to the archive for digitization.
  • Even a single copy can fill a gap in fandom history.
  • Contact us for safe donation, scanning, or return options.

Quick Tips

  • Run a staffed archive intake table with clear signage, such as "Archive Your Conbook Here."
  • Use a simple intake log (event, year, condition, donor) so materials keep their context.
  • Coordinate post-event pickup with venue or hotel staff so abandoned books are recovered quickly.
  • Offer low-friction incentives like raffle entries or donor recognition to boost participation.
  • Review your own collection annually and digitize or sleeve any at-risk books.

Every Page is History

If you have a stack of old conbooks, you’re a guardian of fandom culture. Don’t let that legacy rot in an attic or vanish in a dumpster—help us preserve it for the future.
